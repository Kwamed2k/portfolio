01 — About

Why finance-to-cloud isn't a detour

Most of a cloud engineer's job is invisible until something breaks: reconciling what should have happened against what actually did, tracing a discrepancy back to its source, documenting the fix so it doesn't happen twice. That's also, in plain terms, what a decade in general ledgers and financial systems teaches you.

I spent that decade inside banking and engineering organizations — closing books, managing VAT and tax compliance, and rolling out financial systems that other teams depended on being right. Now I'm rebuilding that same discipline on AWS: automation instead of manual reconciliation, infrastructure instead of ledgers, logs instead of paper trails.

I'm currently completing the AWS re/Start program and preparing for the Cloud Practitioner exam, while building hands-on projects that sit deliberately at the intersection of both worlds — starting with an automated invoice processing pipeline.
